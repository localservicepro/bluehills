Common Questions

01How much does lawn mowing in Pakenham cost?

Standard residential lawn mowing in Pakenham starts at $99+GST per visit for a 2-man crew. Please note clipping removal is not included. Pricing depends on lawn size, frequency, and access. Acreage and commercial properties are quoted on-site so you only pay for the time and equipment your job actually requires.

02Do you mow acreage properties around Nar Nar Goon and Pakenham Upper?

Yes. Acreage mowing is one of our core services across Nar Nar Goon, Pakenham Upper, Tynong, Guys Hill, and Koo Wee Rup. We use ride-on machinery suited to rural blocks from one to ten-plus acres, including paddocks, fence lines, driveways and house surrounds. Bookings can be monthly or seasonal depending on growth.

03Can Blue Hills handle body corporate and strata garden maintenance?

Absolutely. We manage body corporate, strata, and owners’ corporation contracts across Pakenham, Berwick, Narre Warren and Officer. 04How often should I schedule lawn mowing in South-East Melbourne?

Most South-East Melbourne lawns thrive on fortnightly mowing from September to May, and monthly visits from June to August. We’ll recommend the right schedule for your grass type and property after a free on-site assessment.

05Do you offer commercial garden maintenance contracts in Berwick and Narre Warren?
